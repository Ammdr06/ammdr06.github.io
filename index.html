01 / ABOUT

Mathematics with
a wider perspective.

Theoretical and applied
research in mathematics.

I am a doctoral researcher in Mathematics at SRM Institute of Science and Technology. My work spans two distinct directions: the theoretical study of Petri net languages and the application of Petri nets to image segmentation and image encryption.

My theoretical research investigates the generation of partial languages through Petri nets and the extension to Watson–Crick partial Petri net languages, with an emphasis on language classes and closure properties.

In a separate applied direction, I develop Petri net methods for image segmentation and image encryption. The encryption frameworks also incorporate cryptographic hashing and chaotic maps.

I am interested in faculty and postdoctoral opportunities, research collaborations, and related areas of discrete and computational mathematics.
